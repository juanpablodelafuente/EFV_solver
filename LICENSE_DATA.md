# Data licence

The numerical datasets in this repository were produced by the authors and are
licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License
(CC BY 4.0):

<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>

When reusing the data, provide appropriate attribution, identify the archived
version and DOI, link to the licence, and indicate whether changes were made.

The publications cited in the `reference_source` fields define the benchmark
configurations and should be acknowledged when those configurations are reused.
